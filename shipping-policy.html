Shipping Policy

Miami Beauty Lounge is committed to delivering your beauty products safely and on time. This Shipping Policy explains our shipping process, delivery timelines, charges, and important information regarding product deliveries.

1. Order Processing

All orders are carefully verified and processed after successful payment confirmation. Orders are generally prepared within 1–2 business days. Orders placed during weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.

2. Shipping Time

Delivery times depend on your location and courier availability.

  • Local Deliveries: 1–3 Business Days
  • Within India: 3–7 Business Days
  • Remote Locations: 5–10 Business Days
  • International Shipping (if available): 7–15 Business Days

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated during checkout based on your delivery address, order value, and courier partner.

  • Free shipping may be available on selected orders.
  • Additional courier charges may apply for remote areas.
  • Taxes, if applicable, will be shown before payment.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, a confirmation email or SMS containing the tracking number will be sent to you. Customers can use this tracking number to monitor the delivery status through the courier's website.

5. Delivery Attempts

Our delivery partners may attempt delivery multiple times. If the delivery cannot be completed due to an incorrect address or customer unavailability, the package may be returned to us.

6. Damaged or Missing Items

If your package arrives damaged or any items are missing, please contact our customer support within 48 hours of receiving your order. Supporting photographs may be required for verification.

7. Shipping Restrictions

Certain products may not be available for shipping to specific locations due to local regulations, courier limitations, or service availability.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding shipping, delivery status, or courier information, please contact Miami Beauty Lounge through our official phone number, email address, or Contact page.
