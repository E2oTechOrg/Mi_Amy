Payment Policy

Miami Beauty Lounge is committed to providing a secure, transparent, and convenient payment experience. This Payment Policy outlines the accepted payment methods, billing process, refunds, cancellations, and other payment-related terms when purchasing our beauty services or products.

1. Accepted Payment Methods

We accept a variety of secure payment methods to make your shopping and booking experience easy and convenient.

  • Cash Payments
  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
  • Debit & Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay)
  • Net Banking
  • Secure Online Payment Gateway

2. Payment Confirmation

Orders and appointments are confirmed only after successful payment authorization. Once your payment is received, you will receive an email or SMS confirmation containing your booking or order details.

3. Pricing & Taxes

All prices displayed on our website include applicable charges unless otherwise specified. Taxes may be applied according to local regulations and will be shown before completing your payment.

4. Secure Payments

Miami Beauty Lounge does not store your complete debit card, credit card, or banking information. All online payments are processed securely through trusted payment gateway providers using industry-standard encryption technologies.

5. Refund Policy

Payments made for completed beauty services are generally non-refundable. Refund requests for cancelled orders or eligible situations will be reviewed individually and processed according to our Refund Policy.

6. Failed or Declined Transactions

If your payment is declined or fails during the transaction process, no booking or order will be confirmed. Please verify your payment details or contact your bank before attempting another payment.

7. Cancellation & Payment Adjustments

Appointment cancellations must follow our Cancellation Policy. Any eligible payment adjustments or refunds will be processed to the original payment method within the applicable processing period.

8. Fraud Prevention

We reserve the right to refuse or cancel any transaction suspected of fraudulent activity, unauthorized use, or violation of applicable laws. Additional verification may be requested for high-value transactions.

9. Policy Updates

Miami Beauty Lounge may revise this Payment Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Updated versions will be published on this page with immediate effect.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding payments, invoices, refunds, or billing issues, please contact our customer support team through our official phone number, email address, or Contact page.
